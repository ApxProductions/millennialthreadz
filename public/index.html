How ordering works

Four steps

  1. Send a request

    Fill out the quote form with your item, quantity and the date you need it.

  2. Get your price

    We reply with a price and a preview of your logo on the item.

  3. Approve

    Sign off on the design and we get it on the machine.

  4. Pick up or ship

    Pickup by appointment in the LA area, or we ship it to you.

About

Made in LA

Millennial Threadz started in 2020 with one goal: give local businesses, teams and brands merch they're proud to wear. We've stitched and printed for restaurants, auto shops, contractors, schools, softball teams, musicians, beverage brands and independent clothing labels across Southern California.

Every piece is made in-house by the same person who answers your quote. Send us your logo and we'll handle the rest, from the design to the final stitch.
